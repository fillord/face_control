<objective>
Update the two PARTIAL templates — `reports_partial.html` and `timesheet_partial.html` — to the new teal palette, AND restyle the public `error_token.html` token-error page to Inter + teal. The two partials are `{% include %}`-ed fragments inside pages that already extend base.html; they MUST NOT extend base.html and MUST NOT gain `<html>/<head>/<body>` wrappers (doing so causes Jinja2/markup errors). `error_token.html` is a PUBLIC, unauthenticated page (rendered by the `/register/<token>` and `/kiosk/<token>` routes when a token is invalid/expired — there is NO session), so like `kiosk.html`/`login.html`/`register_token.html` it stays standalone per D-12 and is restyled in place rather than converted to a base.html child. Output: `templates/reports_partial.html`, `templates/timesheet_partial.html`, `templates/error_token.html` (all modified, CSS/font only).
</objective>

## register_token.html exclusion (D-12 analogy — documented, not a gap)

`templates/register_token.html` is the PUBLIC, token-gated mobile employee self-registration page rendered by `GET /register/<reg_token>` with NO authenticated session. It is NOT listed in D-14's redesign scope. Per D-12 (public-facing token-flow pages — like `kiosk.html` and `login.html` — stay standalone with their own inline styles because of different layout needs), `register_token.html` is intentionally EXCLUDED from this design overhaul. It keeps its current standalone shell and styles. This exclusion is deliberate, not an oversight: a session-less page cannot render the `session.role` sidebar in base.html. (`error_token.html`, by contrast, IS named in D-14, so it gets a standalone Inter+teal restyle in Task 2 below — without extending base.html, for the same session-less reason.)
</scope_note>
