## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-summary-scope | Spoofing | compute_dept_summary org scope | mitigate | Summary computed for `session["org_id"]` only; org_admin cannot pass another org's id |
| T-03-rate-denom | Tampering | attendance rate computation | mitigate | Denominator is work days (isoweekday in work_days AND not holiday), not calendar days (Pitfall 5) — prevents misleading low rates |
| T-03-holiday-data | Information disclosure / correctness | KZ_HOLIDAYS dates | mitigate | Blocking human checkpoint verifies dates against egov.kz before sign-off (LOW-confidence source A1 + STATE.md blocker) |
| T-03-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | accept | stdlib only — no installs (RESEARCH Package Legitimacy Audit) |
