<objective>
Fix the org-admin Summary tab so it stops switching to the Departments tab.

Purpose: When an org_admin selects a month on the Summary tab and clicks "Показать табель", the page must stay on the Summary tab and show the computed per-department summary — not bounce to Departments.

Output: A one-line correction to the Summary month-picker form action in templates/org_admin.html, then a pm2 restart.
</objective>

<root_cause>
The Summary panel's month-picker form (`templates/org_admin.html` line 172) submits via GET to `action="/org_admin"`.

The Flask route `org_admin_page(tab="depts")` (`app.py` lines 1144-1149) defaults `tab` to `"depts"` when no tab segment is present, so it passes `initial_tab="depts"` to the template.

The bottom-of-page IIFE (`templates/org_admin.html` lines 1182-1185) reads `initial_tab` and calls `switchTab("depts")`, which hides the freshly-computed Summary panel and reveals the Departments panel.

Net effect: every Summary month submission reopens on Departments. The summary data is actually computed and rendered into `panelSummary`, but JS immediately hides it.
</root_cause>
