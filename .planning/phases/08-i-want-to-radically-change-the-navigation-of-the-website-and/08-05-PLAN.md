This plan converts 7 templates across 2 tasks. A split into 05a (timesheet/audit/devices) + 05b (profile/account/403/register) was considered. It was NOT taken because: (a) Tasks 1 and 2 already partition the 7 files into two clean, independently-verifiable batches of 3 and 4; (b) four of the seven (profile/account/403/register) are small form/error pages whose conversion is near-mechanical and low-context; (c) all conversions follow the identical, already-proven pilot recipe from plan 02, so per-file context cost is low. If the executor finds context pressure mid-plan, Task 1 and Task 2 are safe stopping points — each leaves the app importable and its own templates fully converted. Should a split become necessary at execution time, split along the existing Task 1 / Task 2 boundary.
